Privacy Policy for Food Vault

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Overview

Food Vault is a pantry and food management application developed by Refinova. This privacy policy explains how Food Vault handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Data We Collect

Food Vault collects the following data solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Pantry inventory you enter (food items, quantities, expiration dates, storage locations)
  • Recipes, meal plans, and calendar entries you create
  • Barcodes you scan or enter, and food names you search for
  • App settings and preferences (thresholds, warnings, dashboard layout)

How We Use Your Data

All data you enter into Food Vault is stored locally on your device only, used exclusively to power the app's pantry, recipe, and meal-planning features, and never sold or shared with third parties by us.

The only data that leaves your device is sent to Open Food Facts (see below), or data you explicitly choose to export and share yourself.

Data We Do NOT Collect

Food Vault does not collect your name, email, or any personal identity information (unless you choose to enter a display name for the optional Creator Profile feature), location data, contacts or call logs, or analytics/usage tracking of any kind. We do not operate any servers that receive or store your data.

Data Storage

All pantry, recipe, and meal-planning data is stored locally on your device using Hive, a local on-device database. This data never leaves your device unless you use the app's Export feature, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Service: Open Food Facts

When you scan a barcode or search for a food item, Food Vault may send the barcode number or search text to Open Food Facts, a free, open, third-party food product database, in order to retrieve product name, brand, and nutrition information. This is the only outbound network request the app makes to look up food data. Please refer to Open Food Facts' own privacy policy for how they handle that request. You can disable this lookup at any time in Settings.

Export & Import

Food Vault includes an optional Export feature that lets you package your own data (recipes, pantry items, etc.) into a file and share it using your device's native share sheet — for example, to back it up or send a recipe to a friend. This only happens when you explicitly choose to export, and the resulting file is sent only to the destination you pick (e.g. Messages, email, Drive). We do not receive a copy of anything you export.

Permissions We Use

  • Camera — to scan barcodes for adding pantry items. Camera images are processed on-device to read the barcode and are not stored or transmitted.
  • Internet — to look up product data from Open Food Facts.
  • Storage/Files — to save and share exported data files that you initiate.

Children's Privacy

Food Vault does not knowingly collect any personal information from any user, including children under 13. All data remains on the device and is controlled entirely by the device owner.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control at all times. You can delete all app data by uninstalling Food Vault, or clear app data through Android Settings → Apps → Food Vault → Clear Data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, including as new features (such as cloud sync or a recipe marketplace) are added. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
